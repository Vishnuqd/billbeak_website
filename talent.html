Talent Engineering Talent Engineering

We are not recruiters who learned to use new tools. We are Talent Engineers who build better ways to find exceptional people.

Talent Engineers combine verified evidence, human judgement and purpose-built AI systems to turn complex hiring requirements into clear talent intelligence. They build and operate sourcing workflows, research systems, relationship graphs, assessments, dashboards, interview loops and verification processes — then use them to identify the people who match the real demands of the role.

Technology sharpens the signal. Human judgement makes the decision possible.

Contingent Search

One critical role, hired onto your payroll. You pay only when the hire is made.

A dedicated Talent Engineer calibrates the role — a spec of what it genuinely requires, not a wishlist — then builds and runs a purpose-built sourcing workflow until the seat is filled.

What you get
  • Dedicated Talent Engineer on the role
  • Role calibration & real-requirements spec
  • Purpose-built sourcing workflow
  • Verified evidence dossier on every shortlist
  • Structured interview loop & assessment design
  • Offer, negotiation & closing support
What you won’t get
  • A team on our payroll — this hire joins yours
  • Coverage of multiple concurrent roles
  • A flood of unvetted CVs — shortlist, not volume

Project Subscription

You bring the project. We deploy the team — on our payroll — until it ships.

Zero hiring, zero severance, zero bench. A Talent-Engineer-assembled team scoped to your exact project — scaling with its phases and winding down cleanly at completion.

What you get
  • A team assembled for your exact scope
  • Every member Billbeak-verified in our 3 domains
  • Salaries, payroll & compliance carried by Billbeak
  • Underperformers replaced at no extra cost
  • Scales up & down with project phases
  • One subscription invoice — no per-head haggling
What you won’t get
  • Employees on your payroll — the team stays ours
  • Generic body-shopping outside our domains
  • Open-ended staffing with no defined end state
  • Direct hiring without a conversion agreement

Access Young Talent

Twenty Billbeak-Verified fresh graduates per role — selected on demonstrated capability, not CV keywords.

Every candidate arrives pre-assessed — projects, motivation, career drive and communication, verified before you meet anyone. Review, shortlist and interview from a private dashboard.

What you get
  • 20 fresh graduates matched to one specific role

  • All assessments completed before you meet anyone
  • Private employer dashboard to review & shortlist

  • Hire 1 or all 20 — the engagement doesn’t change
  • Internship-to-hire conversion included
What you won’t get
  • Experienced or senior-level candidates
  • One batch reused across multiple roles
  • Raw CV dumps — verified or it isn’t sent
